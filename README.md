# HR_Analytics_Dashboard
The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze workforce demographics, compensation trends, employee distribution, leave balances, and organizational growth. 
## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Monitor workforce size and employee demographics.
- Analyze salary distribution across departments and job roles.
- Evaluate employee leave balances and utilization.
- Track workforce growth trends over time.
- Identify top and bottom salary earners.
- Support strategic HR planning and talent management decisions.
## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Headcount
- Average Salary
- Average Leave Balance
- Employees with Leave Balance Above Threshold
- Total Compensation Overview
- Workforce Growth Metrics
- Workforce Analytics
## 👨‍💼 Employee Distribution by Job Role

- Identify departments with the largest workforce.
## 👥 Gender Diversity Analysis

## 🎂 Employee Age Distribution
- Visualize workforce age demographics.
- Identify dominant age groups and workforce composition.
## 🎓 Salary vs Qualification Analysis
- Compare compensation across educational qualifications.
- Analyze salary trends by degree level and certification.

## 📈 Key Insights Generated
- Workforce consists of 161 employees across multiple business functions.
- Female employees represent a slightly larger portion of the workforce than male employees.
- Significant salary variation exists across departments and qualification levels.
- Certain job roles maintain higher average leave balances.
- Workforce growth has accelerated in recent years.
- Compensation trends can be analyzed at employee, department, and qualification levels.
## 🚀 Business Value
This dashboard enables HR leaders and management teams to gain visibility into workforce composition, compensation structures, employee demographics, and organizational growth. By centralizing critical HR metrics into an interactive reporting solution, decision-makers can improve workforce planning, optimize compensation strategies, support diversity initiatives, and enhance employee engagement.
